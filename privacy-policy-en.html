Hori+Verti Cam Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Hori+Verti Cam ("the app") is a camera app that records a landscape video and a portrait video (or takes a landscape photo and a portrait photo) at the same time. The developer does not collect any personal information, and your photos and videos never leave your device.

1. Information we collect

The developer collects no personal information. The app contains no ads, analytics, or accounts.

The unlimited recording purchase (in-app purchase) is processed by the Google Play billing system. Payment details and purchase records are handled by Google; the developer does not receive your payment method or other personal information. The app only checks whether the purchase was made. The Google Play Billing Library may communicate with Google servers to provide this, subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Permissions and why they are used

PermissionPurpose
CameraTaking photos and videos, and showing the preview
MicrophoneRecording sound with the video
Storage (Android 9 and below)Saving videos to Movies/HoriVerti and photos to Pictures/HoriVerti on your device
NotificationsShowing save/convert progress
Foreground serviceFinishing saving/converting videos even if you leave the app
Billing (Google Play), InternetThe unlimited recording in-app purchase. Used by the Google Play Billing Library; never used to send photos or videos

Photos, video and audio are stored only on your device (in your gallery) and are never sent to anyone, including the developer.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Videos: Movies/HoriVerti, photos: Pictures/HoriVerti in your gallery. You can delete them from your gallery at any time.
  • App settings such as quality, and whether you bought unlimited recording: the app's private storage. Removed when you uninstall the app (your purchase stays on your Google account and is restored automatically when you reinstall).

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not share any information with third parties. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.

5. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
